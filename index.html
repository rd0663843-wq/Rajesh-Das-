Built around focus, not noise.

My desk has the same three things on it most days: a laptop, a notebook, and a stack of books on habits and deep work. That's not decoration — it's roughly how I try to run projects: fewer distractions, clearer priorities, steady output.

I got into digital marketing because I liked the mix of creative work and measurable results — a post, a page, or a campaign either connects with people or it doesn't, and you can tell. I bring that same directness to how I work with clients: honest timelines, plain explanations, and no promises I can't keep.

If you're a small business or an independent creator who wants someone who actually does the work — rather than manages a team doing the work — that's where I fit best.
